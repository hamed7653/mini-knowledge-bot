SERVICES & PRODUCT OVERVIEW — ARVAN ENGINEERING & CONSTRUCTION CO.
======================================================================

ABOUT ARVAN ENGINEERING
------------------------
Founded in 1382 (2003), Arvan Engineering & Construction Co. is one of Iran's leading EPC contractors with over 20 years of experience delivering complex industrial projects. With a workforce of 1,200+ engineers and specialists, Arvan has completed more than 80 projects with a combined contract value exceeding USD 4 billion.
