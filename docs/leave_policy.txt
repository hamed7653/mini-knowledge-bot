EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY — ARVAN ENGINEERING & CONSTRUCTION CO.
HR-POL-002 | Revision 3 | Effective: 1403/01/01
======================================================================

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
---------------
- All permanent employees are entitled to 26 working days of annual leave per year, per Iranian Labor Law (Article 64).
- Project-site employees working in remote or hazardous locations receive an additional 6 days (total: 32 days).
- Leave must be planned and coordinated with the direct supervisor and submitted via the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for leaves up to 3 days, and 14 days in advance for longer periods.
- Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days per year. Any balance beyond 10 days will be compensated monetarily at year-end.

2. SICK LEAVE
-------------
- Employees are entitled to unlimited sick leave covered by Social Security (Tamin Ejtemaei), subject to a medical certificate from an approved physician.
- For absences of 1–2 days, a self-declaration form (HR-F-009) is acceptable. For 3 or more consecutive days, a formal medical certificate must be submitted to HR within 5 working days.
- Sick leave does not reduce annual leave balance.

3. MATERNITY & PATERNITY LEAVE
-------------------------------
- Maternity leave: 9 months fully paid, in accordance with the 1401 amendment to Iranian Labor Law.
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks (10 working days) paid leave upon birth of a child.
- Adoption leave: equivalent to maternity/paternity leave applies for legal adoption of children under 6 years old.

4. EMERGENCY / BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
----------------------------------
- Death of first-degree relatives (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 3 working days paid leave.
- Death of second-degree relatives (grandparent, in-laws): 1 working day paid leave.
- Emergency leave (fire, flood, accident) is granted at the discretion of the Department Head, up to 2 days, and must be documented within 3 working days.

5. UNPAID LEAVE
---------------
- Employees with more than 2 years of service may apply for unpaid leave up to 30 days per year for personal reasons, subject to approval by the Department Head and HR Director.
- Unpaid leave does not affect seniority calculations but does reduce social security contribution periods.

6. NATIONAL & RELIGIOUS HOLIDAYS
----------------------------------
- All official national holidays and public holidays declared by the Iranian government are recognized. Typically 27 days per year.
- Employees required to work on holidays will receive 140% of their daily rate as compensation (Labor Law Article 62).

7. LEAVE REQUEST PROCESS
-------------------------
Step 1: Submit request via HR Self-Service Portal (hr.arvan-eng.com)
Step 2: Supervisor approval within 2 working days
Step 3: HR confirmation and calendar update
Step 4: Notification sent to employee and payroll team

For emergency or same-day requests, contact HR directly at hr@arvan-eng.com or extension 1200.

8. POLICY VIOLATIONS
---------------------
Unapproved absence for more than 3 consecutive working days will be treated as unauthorized leave and may result in disciplinary action per Disciplinary Code DC-001.
